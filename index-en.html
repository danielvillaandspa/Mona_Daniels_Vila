Our Story

A Designed
Retreat

A beautifully designed villa offering comfortable and well-equipped rooms alongside a luxurious indoor spa complex. The space was designed with clean lines and warm touches to create an intimate and peaceful experience for every guest.

The villa is fully fenced ensuring complete privacy. Includes two spacious lounges, a fully equipped kitchen, a manicured garden with BBQ corner, ping pong table and sun loungers.
